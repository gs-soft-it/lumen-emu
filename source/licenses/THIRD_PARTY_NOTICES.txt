source/cpu/qemu/source-lock.json. Lumen selects GPL version 2 where the
CPU/TCG closure permits it and preserves other applicable per-file licenses.

from gettext 1.0 as static PIC dependencies. They preserve their upstream
license texts and notices; gettext command-line programs are not linked.

source/cpu/qemu/dependency-lock.json. The Android recipe records actual
compiled/header preambles and toolchain notices with a hash manifest under
assets/Legal/QEMU-TCG. QEMU's own source is prepared outside this repository;
no guest firmware, guest OS or game is part of the provider. Complete
corresponding source, dependency notices and applicable relinking materials
remain obligations for any future distribution. This optional provider does
not certify the full Lumen binary's license compatibility or distribution.

regressions in source/gen4/gen5/tests/Sdl3AndroidWaitTests.py. The SDL license text is packaged as
Legal/ps5/SDL3/LICENSE.txt.

Active PS4 core (source/gen4)
----------------------------
The shadPS4-derived core, through the shadps4-arm64 fork, is pinned to
be6bc2e9c60799e071dd2fafa6216e8d80ec619c.
Source provenance is recorded in source/gen4/UPSTREAM.json. Original copyright
notices and GPL-2.0-or-later declarations are retained; this integration
selects GPL version 2. Lumen supplies the dashboard, managed entrypoints,
shared CPU boundary and native Metal host adapter. The complete dependency
pin list, component notice texts and embedded font notices are preserved
under source/licenses/gen4 and packaged under Legal/gen4. This includes
the active direct FEX pin f2b679f6028ce1c38875233aecfcf5d3f8ebecec,
shared by Android Gen4 and direct Gen5. The e17fdf9 FEX pin above covers
the retained legacy/facade paths. The manifest
records the exact notice bytes. Host adapters are modified source, not an
unmodified upstream binary release.

Metal software tessellator
--------------------------

Mesa 26.1.0 src/poly/cl/tessellator.h and the associated parameter wrappers.
The algorithm is the Microsoft D3D11 Fixed Function Tessellator Reference
(May 7, 2012). Original source SHA-256:
