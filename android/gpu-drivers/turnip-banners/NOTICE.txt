Mesa Turnip Banners A8xx — pinned upstream prebuilt
===================================================

This package preserves the unmodified Adreno A8xx Android/AdrenoTools driver
from The412Banner/Banners-Turnip release v27.0.0-20261018-r3.
Release: https://github.com/The412Banner/Banners-Turnip/releases/tag/v27.0.0-20261018-r3
Build recipe revision: 7be393dd160769eb36d9962a2605ed18fd90c2ed
Mesa revision: c823a83e8f373a5c36c2b9ed1d7dc98a4f3360ee
Driver SHA-256: 06f01b7063c857f893219560a025f683a6edba11a2452675f54dbb8e1b32b737

Original work includes Mesa and its contributors, The412Banner's build
integration, whitebelyash's turnip/gen8 stack, and the Max/MaxsTechReview
WinNative series identified by the upstream patches/common/SOURCE record.
The recipe, source record and applied A8xx patches are retained without
modification in provenance/banners. The upstream workflow is stored under
github-workflows instead of .github/workflows so Android asset packaging
retains it; its bytes and source URL remain unchanged.
The build recipe selects a8xx_gen8.patch,
a8xx_shared_mem.py and WinNative patches 0001 through 0005; it omits 0006.

The Banners repository distributes its recipe under GPL-3.0 and its SOURCE
record identifies the Max/WinNative patch series as GPL-3.0. The complete
repository licence is retained at licenses/Banners-Turnip-GPL-3.0.txt.
Mesa's own component terms and per-file copyrights remain applicable;
licenses/mesa preserves the licence texts and licence description at the
recorded Mesa revision. The driver must not be described as MIT-only.
The upstream recipe names Android NDK r29 and statically links the C++
runtime. Broad NDK r29 notices are retained under licenses/android-ndk-r29.

No Lumen modification or rebuild of this driver is performed. The upstream
binary archive contains only the shared library and meta.json, so Lumen adds
these source, attribution and licence records alongside it without altering
the driver bytes. source-lock.json identifies all retained materials.
The complete upstream source trees remain available at the recorded
repositories/revisions; the retained recipe is not a complete Mesa checkout.

The exact source-to-binary reproduction and full transitive binary licence
closure have not been independently verified. Preserving these records is not
a determination of combined-binary licence compatibility or redistribution
qualification. Existing project licence limitations remain applicable.

This A8xx package is separate from upstream's Standard A6xx/A7xx build.
Its meta.json declares Android API 28 and Adreno 810/812/825/829/830/840;
those upstream declarations do not qualify every device or emulator runtime.
