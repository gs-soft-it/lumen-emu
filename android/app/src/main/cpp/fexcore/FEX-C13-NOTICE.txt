PS5 candidate: C13 FEX integration, 2026-10-18

When this notice is packaged, the isolated PS5 runtime uses FEX commit
f2b679f6028ce1c38875233aecfcf5d3f8ebecec from https://github.com/FEX-Emu/FEX,
under its retained MIT license. This replaces the legacy PS5 e17fdf9
provenance described in FEXCORE-NOTICE.md for this candidate. Gen4 retains
its separately documented direct FEX integration.

Lumen's local PS5 overlay adapts translated-block lifecycle and retry metadata,
app-owned JIT symbol output, conditional-branch mutation safepoints,
independent SSE/AVX denormal modes, and the exclusive-publication embedding ABI.
The integration preserves the PS5 HLE/VM facade. Interval instrumentation
is unavailable. Persistent
translation disk cache and 16-KiB host pages are not qualified or admitted.
This is a local derivative, not an upstream FEX release.
